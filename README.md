# Insurance Fraud Risk & Claims Monitoring Dashboard

Power BI dashboard monitoring £53M in insurance claims, identifying fraud patterns across 247 fraudulent claims (24.7% fraud rate) and enabling data-driven intervention strategies.

![Fraud Monitoring Dashboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f6109331-f03a-435f-9de3-d7ddc98e34ae)

## Project Objectives
- Monitor fraud patterns in real-time across insurance claims
- Identify high-risk segments for targeted fraud prevention
- Balance fraud detection with operational efficiency
- Provide executive-ready insights for strategic decision-making

## Key Insights Delivered

### Fraud Pattern Analysis
- **£15M in fraudulent claims** identified (24.7% fraud rate)
- **Single vehicle collisions** showed highest fraud risk
- **Males** exhibited significantly higher fraud rates (26% vs 23%), requiring enhanced verification protocols
- **18-24 age group** identified as highest-risk demographic (23.19%)

### Geographic & Temporal Trends
- **Arlington** flagged at 29% fraud rate for prioritised investigations
- **March fraud spike** detected (23% → 33%), enabling rapid response
- **Monthly trend analysis** showed seasonal patterns requiring proactive measures

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Executive Summary
- Total payout (£53M)
- Fraud share of cost (£15M)
- Fraudulent claims percentage (24.7%)
- Total fraud claim count (247)
- Cost multiplier (1.2X)

### 2. Risk Segmentation Analysis
- **By Incident Type**: Single vehicle collisions identified as highest risk
- **By Demographics**: Gender and age band analysis
- **By Location**: Geographic fraud rate comparison
- **By Time**: Monthly fraud trend monitoring

### 3. Actionable Insights
- Enhanced verification protocols for males
- Extra verification required for 18-24 age band
- Arlington prioritised for investigations
- Real-time spike detection (March 33% alert)
